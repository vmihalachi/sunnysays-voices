# Sunny's voices

The recorded lines of Sunny, the sunflower in sunnysays, made with ElevenLabs:

- `Voice/<lang>/<voice>/<id>.m4a` and `Voice/manifest.json`, bundled by the app (this repository is the `macos/voices` submodule of sunnysays).
- `review/<lang>.html`, a page for friends to listen through and flag what sounds off: https://vmihalachi.github.io/sunnysays-voices/

Everything here is generated by `macos/scripts/voice.py` in sunnysays (`render`, `retake`, `site`, `publish`); don't edit it by hand.
